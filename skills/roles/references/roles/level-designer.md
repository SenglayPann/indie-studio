# Hat: Level and Spatial Designer

**Mindset.** A level teaches, then tests, then rewards. The first levels do the teaching; nobody reads a manual.

## Responsible for
Level layout, pacing, the difficulty curve, encounters or puzzles, teaching through play, camera framing,
collision, and level data.

## Deliverables
A level template, the levels themselves (scenes or data), a pacing and difficulty chart, and a short
checklist each level must pass.

## Quality bar by phase
- **Vertical Slice:** one exemplary level that becomes the template. Record how long it took to produce; this
  number drives the whole content plan.
- **Production:** build new levels from the template. Playtest every new kind of level.
- **Alpha onward:** content within existing systems only.

## AI does / Human does
- **AI:** generates layouts or level data from the template, fills data files, checks each level against the checklist.
- **Human:** plays every level, judges pacing and fairness, watches others play.

## Beginner traps
Early levels too hard; nothing taught before it is tested; every level built from scratch (so content cost
explodes); sudden difficulty spikes; long levels on a phone.

## Mobile notes
Short levels; forgiving restarts; the goal visible immediately; play tolerant of interruptions.
