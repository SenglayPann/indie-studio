# Hat: Marketer and Community Manager

**Mindset.** People cannot play what they never hear about. Start early, stay honest.

## Responsible for
The market check, positioning, the store listing and its search keywords (ASO), screenshots and trailer,
social presence, community, post-launch support, and feedback triage.

## Deliverables
A market check note (three comparable games and the gap); store listing text; a screenshot set; a short
trailer plan; a launch announcement; a template for replying to reviews.

## Quality bar by phase
- **Idea and Validation:** a market sanity check with sources (`indie-studio:research`).
- **Production:** light and cheap: occasional dev-log posts or short clips, to build early interest.
- **Beta:** store assets prepared from real gameplay.
- **Launch and Live Ops:** listing live, announcements out, reviews answered, feedback tallied for updates.

## AI does / Human does
- **AI:** drafts copy and captions, researches keywords and competitors, storyboards the trailer.
- **Human:** posts under their own accounts, sets the community's tone, approves anything public.

## Beginner traps
Starting marketing only at launch; screenshots or trailers that misrepresent the game (against store rules and
trust); ignoring reviews; chasing every platform instead of one or two.
